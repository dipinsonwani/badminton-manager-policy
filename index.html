Badminton Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 September 2026

In short: Badminton Manager does not collect, send or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Badminton Manager app ("the app", "we", "us") for Android and iOS treats information. The app is developed and published by Dipin Sonwani, an individual developer. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or device data. The app has no user accounts and does not connect to the internet to send data anywhere. It does not include analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking tools, and it does not use advertising identifiers.

2. Information stored on your device

To work, the app saves the information you enter in a private file on your device:

WhatExamples
PlayersNames you type, and optionally gender, playing hand and notes
TournamentsTournament names, format, rules, notes and dates
MatchesScores, results and match notes

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share or back it up (see sections 3 and 4). We cannot see it and we have no copy of it. If you enter other people's names, please make sure they are happy for you to record their results.

3. When you share information

When you tap Share result, Share standings or Back up, the app opens your device's standard share sheet with the relevant text. You choose where it goes (for example a messaging app, email or notes). Whatever app you share to handles the data under its own privacy policy. We do not receive a copy.

4. Device backups

Your operating system may include the app's data in your normal device backups. On Android this is Android Backup to your Google account; on iOS it is iCloud Backup or a computer backup. These backups are managed by Google or Apple under your account settings and their privacy policies, not by us. You can turn them off in your device settings.

5. Permissions

The app does not request any special permissions. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

6. Links to other websites

The Settings screen includes an optional "Buy me a coffee" link. Tapping it opens the Buy Me a Coffee website in your browser. Any information you give there, including payment details, is handled by that service under its own privacy policy. The app does not receive or store any of it.

7. Sharing with third parties

We do not share, sell, rent or trade any information with third parties, because we do not collect any. There are no third-party SDKs in the app that collect data.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. You can remove it at any time:

  • Delete a single player or tournament from within the app.
  • Use Settings → Delete all data to erase everything.
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

9. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage, which the operating system protects from other apps. Keep your device protected with a passcode for extra security. If you export a backup, keep that file or message somewhere safe.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, none is sent to us.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Because all data stays on your device, you can exercise these rights directly in the app. We hold no personal data about you. If you have any questions, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the app gains new features. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how data is handled, we will say so in the app's release notes.
